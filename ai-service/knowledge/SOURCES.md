### Original poultry files
`Aviagen_Broiler_Growth_and_Feed_Standards.md` was DELETED on 2026-10-07 at the owner's request and replaced by `poultry_broiler_ross308_targets.md`. `FAO_Poultry_Diseases_and_Management.md` is kept, but it is a short summary with unclear provenance, not the official FAO publication. Checked against the official Ross 2022 table:
- Daily feed figures and day 28 to 42 weights are close to Ross (day 28 about 1616 g, 145 g/day).
- Early weights in the old file are lower than Ross: day 7 old 180 to 200 g vs Ross 213 g; day 14 old 450 to 500 g vs Ross 533 g; day 21 old 900 to 1000 g vs Ross 1012 g. Day 1 to 7 intake 15 to 30 g/day vs Ross day 7 of 35 g.
- Drug doses (amprolium 0.024%, toltrazuril 7 mg/kg) and vaccination days in the old FAO-style file could not be verified and differ from the Nigerian schedules found. Do not quote them as official. Replace or confirm with a vet before the demo.
- Note on the seed data: `backend/seed.py` treats 48 kg/day for 500 broilers at day 28 as a "normal feeding rate" (about 96 g per bird). The Ross objective at day 28 is 145 g per bird (about 72.5 kg for 500 birds), so the demo farm is already below the objective before the drop to 32 kg. Fine for a demo, but be ready to explain it.

## Safety rules for the chatbot
Never state a firm diagnosis or give drug doses from these documents. Name the likely causes, give safe management steps, ask follow-up questions, and refer to a vet or the state veterinary office. Suspected PPR, ASF, FMD, LSD, CBPP, RVF and Newcastle disease should be reported to the veterinary authority.
